# 0–30 Day Financial Plan — Bootstrapped (Morris Law Kernel v3.5 + IP System)

## Overall Strategy
Focus on **speed to revenue** and **proof of value** with minimal spend. Goal: Generate early revenue or strong signals for licensing/buyout within 30 days.

## Week 1 (Days 1–7): Foundation & Visibility
- Launch public GitHub repo with clean README and demo.
- Post 7 daily value-first posts across X, LinkedIn, Reddit, Facebook (see promo document).
- Reach out to 20–30 targeted people on LinkedIn (see outreach list).
- Set up basic landing page (Carrd or similar) explaining the system.
- **Target:** 500+ impressions, 50+ profile visits, first conversations.

## Week 2 (Days 8–14): Proof + Early Conversations
- Release short demo video (mastering + IP verification flow).
- Offer limited "early access" or pilot partnerships (free or low-cost for feedback + testimonial).
- Continue daily posting + targeted outreach.
- **Target:** 3–5 serious conversations with potential licensees or investors.

## Week 3 (Days 15–21): Monetization Experiments
- Open paid early access / licensing conversations.
- Create simple one-page licensing terms (engine + verification API).
- Explore small paid pilots ($1k–5k range) for integration support.
- **Target:** At least 1 paid pilot or strong LOI.

## Week 4 (Days 22–30): Close or Escalate
- Follow up aggressively on warm leads.
- Prepare short data room (White Paper + Pitch Deck + demo + code access under NDA).
- Decide path: 
  - Bootstrap via early licensing/paid pilots, or
  - Raise small angel/strategic round, or
  - Position for acquisition / larger licensing deal.

## Revenue Targets (Conservative Bootstrapped)
- **Best case (30 days):** 1–2 paid pilots ($3k–10k total) + strong acquisition interest.
- **Realistic:** 3–5 serious licensing conversations + 1–2 LOIs.
- **Minimum viable:** Public validation + 50+ engaged followers in target audience.

## Key Metrics to Track
- GitHub stars / forks
- Demo video views + engagement
- LinkedIn connection acceptance + reply rate
- Number of serious conversations started
- Pilot / LOI pipeline value

## Recommended Tools (Low/No Cost)
- Carrd or Framer for landing page
- Loom for demo videos
- Notion or Google Docs for data room
- Stripe for future payments
- Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy for simple productized licensing (future)

---

**This plan is designed to create momentum and optionality within 30 days while bootstrapped.**
