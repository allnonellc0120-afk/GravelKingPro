# PITCH DECK VISUAL DESIGN GUIDE — Morris Law Kernel v3.5

## Overall Style Recommendations
- Dark theme with green/cyan accents (GravelKing brand)
- Clean, modern, professional
- Use high-quality audio waveform and circuit-style visuals
- Minimal text per slide — big bold headlines
- Include screenshots of the Streamlit app and verification report

## Slide-by-Slide Visual Ideas

**Slide 1: Title**
- Background: Dark gradient
- Large title text
- Subtle waveform graphic
- GravelKing logo + tagline

**Slide 2: The Problem**
- Split screen: Left = expensive black-box mastering (icon), Right = weak watermark (icon with "X")
- Bullet points with icons
- Red "X" accents for pain points

**Slide 3: The Solution**
- Central hero image: "Before → After" mastering + IP stamp
- Three icons: Mastering Gear, Shield (IP), Lock (Crypto)
- "One Integrated System" headline

**Slide 4: How It Works**
- Clean flow diagram:
  Audio → MLK v3 Kernel → Robust Embed → Cryptographic Record → Blockchain Anchor
- Use arrows and numbered steps
- Show a sample verification report screenshot

**Slide 5: The Moat (Competitive Advantage)**
- Comparison table (columns: Competitor 1, Competitor 2, Morris Law Kernel)
- Highlight "First & Only" with green checkmarks
- Technical moat icons (Numba, Crypto, Robust Watermark)

**Slide 6: Market Opportunity**
- Market size numbers with icons (Independent Creators, DAWs, Sync Libraries, etc.)
- Simple pie or bar chart showing addressable market

**Slide 7: Business Model**
- Revenue streams with icons: Licensing, API Access, Premium Features, Strategic Partnerships
- Simple revenue model graphic

**Slide 8: Traction & Roadmap**
- Current status bullets + checkmarks
- Roadmap timeline (Q3 2026 – Q4 2027)

**Slide 9: The Ask / Team**
- Clear ask: "Licensing partners, strategic investors, potential acquirers"
- Founder photo + bio
- Contact info

**Slide 10: Thank You**
- Large "Questions?" 
- Contact info + QR code to demo or GitHub
- GravelKing logo

---

## Design Tips
- Use Canva, Gamma, or Pitch for quick creation
- Keep color scheme consistent (dark background, cyan/green accents)
- Include 1–2 actual screenshots of the working system
- Keep text large and minimal — visuals carry the story

**This design guide makes the pitch deck visually compelling and investor-ready.**
