# GravelKing Pro Launch Walkthrough — Master Copy

## Positioning
GravelKing Pro is a creator-audio workspace for provenance, production, and release. The launch message is simple: make the song, keep the proof.

## Product sequence
1. Open the live app at https://gravelkingpro.com.
2. Enter JAX Songwriting with a persistent session.
3. Generate a lyric response and review its immutable AI snapshot.
4. Edit lyric lines directly; those edits are the source of human authorship credit.
5. Use “Regenerate (Keep Human Lines)” to change untouched AI lines while preserving human lines verbatim.
6. Send the exact final generator lyrics into Song Generator.
7. Generate an unmastered take.
8. Use paid MLK v3.5 mastering as a separate step when the track is ready.
9. Review the final lyric hash, authorship score, ledger, and certificate label.
10. Use Vocal Booth, karaoke, download, and certificate paths according to the account tier.

## Proof language
Use the exact phrase “Certified Final Rendered Lyrics.” Do not imply that an authorship certificate proves who physically typed every character; it records the evidence trail represented by the workflow.

## Campaign CTA
Primary CTA: Try GravelKing Pro and keep the proof.
Secondary CTA: Start with one creator, one song, and one auditable handoff.

## Claims discipline
Do not publish fabricated testimonials, unsupported performance percentages, unverified revenue claims, or claims that AI output is automatically copyrightable. Describe the product behavior, the evidence trail, and the pilot measurement plan.
