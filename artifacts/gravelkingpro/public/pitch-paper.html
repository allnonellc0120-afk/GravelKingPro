What We Do

The Platform That Gives Artists Proof They Made Their Music

GravelKingPro is the world's first music production platform with built-in intellectual property protection. Every tool an artist uses — from recording to mixing to writing lyrics — generates a forensic record that proves their human creative contribution. When that contribution reaches the legal threshold, the platform issues a certified IP document they can use to defend their rights.

The Platform

Ten Tools. One Workflow.

Every tool in GravelKingPro is designed to serve one goal: help artists create great music and walk away owning it. Here's what the platform does, in plain English.

✍️
Songwriting Studio Pro
What it does: Writes lyrics with you and tracks your contribution
The artist describes their song concept, and the AI writes a starting draft. Then, as the artist rewrites, edits, and personalizes the lyrics, the platform tracks exactly how much of the final song came from the human vs. the AI. That percentage becomes part of the IP record.
🎚️
Professional Mixing Studio (DAW) Pro
What it does: A full recording studio in a web browser
Artists can mix, layer, and edit their tracks directly in GravelKingPro — no software to download, no studio to book. Every adjustment they make (volume, effects, arrangement) is logged as a creative action that contributes to their authorship score.
⚖️
MLK V3 Authorship Engine Core Technology
What it does: Measures how much of the music is human-made
This is the technology that makes GravelKingPro unique and irreplaceable. It runs quietly in the background during every session, comparing what the artist does against the original AI-generated baseline, and outputs a score from 0 to 100 percent. This score is the legal foundation for the IP certificate. No other music platform has this.
🏛️
IP Embed Certificate Flagship Feature
What it does: Issues a legal proof-of-ownership document
When an artist's score crosses 25%, GravelKingPro issues a certificate — a tamper-proof, timestamped digital document tied to their specific track and session. They can post it on their website, attach it to streaming profiles, or use it in a legal dispute. Think of it as a notary stamp for music. No other platform offers this.
🎤
Vocal Booth Pro
What it does: A practice and recording environment for singers
Artists can sing over any instrumental track with the lyrics automatically scrolling on screen in real time — like a professional karaoke booth. It also monitors the live vocal signal for level and quality. Great for rehearsal, content creation, or capturing a vocal performance.
🥁
Beat Maker Pro
What it does: Creates original beats using AI
Artists describe the style, tempo, and feel of beat they want, and the platform generates a custom beat. The beat is then loaded directly into the mixing studio for the artist to customize further — and every customization counts toward their authorship score.
💿
Track Mastering Pro
What it does: Makes tracks ready for streaming services
After mixing, artists submit their track for mastering — the final audio polish step required before releasing on Spotify, Apple Music, or any platform. The system automatically sets the loudness to streaming standards and delivers a release-ready file with a technical report.
📚
Track Library All Tiers
What it does: Stores everything in one organized place
Every project, stem, mix, master file, and IP certificate lives in the Track Library. Artists can access their entire creative history from any device, download any file at any time, and manage their IP portfolio as it grows.
🔬
Node Auditor Enterprise · $499/mo
What it does: Audits entire music catalogs for IP documentation
Designed for record labels and music distributors. Node Auditor can process thousands of tracks at once, analyzing each one for human authorship and generating IP certificates across an entire catalog. This is the enterprise revenue engine — a single label contract is worth more than twelve individual Pro subscriptions.
🏷️
Label Hub Enterprise
What it does: Gives labels a command center for their artists
A management portal where record labels can see all their artists, review IP certificate status across their roster, submit bulk track orders, and track the authorship scores for every song in their catalog. Built specifically for teams, not individual users.
