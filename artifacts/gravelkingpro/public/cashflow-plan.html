Revenue Model

Three Drivers. One Compounding Network.

The cash flow model is structured around three distinct revenue streams that are not independent — they reinforce each other. Every mastered track generates a cert. Every cert in the database increases the authority of the verification network. Every enterprise verification call monetizes that authority. The network effect is the moat.

Driver 1 — Automated Mastering Fees: The primary acquisition engine. Free tier converts to $9.99/mo Pro. High volume, high conversion intent (users who upload a track are already committed). Every Pro subscriber generates certs that grow the database.

Driver 2 — Enterprise Catalog Audit Subscriptions: The high-margin, high-LTV anchor. Labels and distributors auditing AI-assisted catalogs. $499/mo Starter → $2,499/mo Pro → $25K–$100K/yr licensing deals. 10 enterprise customers equals the ARR of 500 Pro subscribers.

Driver 3 — Verification API Call Volumes: The compounding revenue floor. Third parties (attorneys, platforms, A&Rs, distributors) pay per verification call. $0.10/call standalone, $0.05/call in bulk packages. As the cert database grows, call volume grows without additional customer acquisition cost.

Unit Economics

Pro Subscriber

  • Price: $9.99/mo
  • Avg lifespan: 18 months
  • LTV: ~$180
  • CAC: ~$0 (organic SEO + community)
  • LTV:CAC: ∞ (organic)
  • Gross margin: ~98% (no per-user infra cost)
  • Break-even: Month 1 of subscription

Enterprise Starter

  • Price: $499/mo
  • Avg lifespan: 24 months (contract)
  • LTV: ~$11,976
  • CAC: ~$500–$2K (direct outreach, demos)
  • LTV:CAC: 6–24×
  • Gross margin: ~96%
  • Break-even: Month 1–4 of contract

Verify API

  • Price: $0.10/call (bulk: $0.05)
  • CAC: $0 (passive — certs drive demand)
  • Gross margin: ~99.5%
  • Revenue tied to cert database size
  • Floor: grows even with 0 new subscribers
  • Ceiling: every cert ever issued generates
  • potential future API calls indefinitely

Chain-of-Title License (Y2+)

  • Price: $25K–$100K/yr
  • CAC: $1K–$5K (enterprise sales motion)
  • LTV: $25K–$300K+ (multi-year)
  • LTV:CAC: 5–60×
  • 1 deal = 2,500–10,000 Pro subscribers equiv.
  • Gross margin: ~99% (API infra is sunk cost)
  • Not modeled in base case above
