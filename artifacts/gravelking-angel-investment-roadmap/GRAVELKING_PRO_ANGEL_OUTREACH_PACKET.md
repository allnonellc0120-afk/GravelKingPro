# GravelKing Pro — Angel Investor Outreach Packet

## Important use note
This list is a prospecting map, not a list of confirmed investors. Investment focus, fund status, and check size can change. Verify the current public profile before contacting anyone. Never claim that a person has agreed to invest.

## The ask
Request: **$250,000–$500,000 total** from a small group of cash investors. Individual target asks are intentionally smaller: $25,000–$100,000 for angels/operators, $50,000–$250,000 for specialist funds, and larger asks only for strategic or institutional prospects.

## Email template
Subject: GravelKing Pro — creator-audio provenance and a focused $250K–$500K angel round

Hi [Name],

I’m building GravelKing Pro through GravelKing Productions: a live creator-audio workspace combining JAX songwriting sessions, immutable AI lyric snapshots, direct human lyric editing, authorship tracking, creator audio tools, unmastered generation, and paid MLK v3.5 mastering.

The product is live at https://gravelkingpro.com. The current production snapshot is 123 users, 41 tracks, and 27 certificate records. I am raising $250,000–$500,000 in cash capital to prove paid retention, run one label/studio/school/publisher pilot, reconcile the IP and billing data room, and build a repeatable acquisition channel.

I’m asking whether you would consider a [recommended ask] investment, or introduce me to someone who would. I can send the product demo, B2B partnership brief, financial appraisal, and 30-day pilot plan.

Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation next week?

Best,
Kevin
GravelKing Productions
