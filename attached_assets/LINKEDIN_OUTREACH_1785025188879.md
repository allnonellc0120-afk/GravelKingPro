# LinkedIn Outreach — Targeted Messages + Specific Contacts

## How to Use This Document
1. Search the names/roles below on LinkedIn.
2. Verify they are still in relevant positions.
3. Use the copy-paste message templates (customize slightly for warmth).

## Recommended Search Terms (Run These on LinkedIn)

### DAW & Audio Software Companies
- "Head of Audio" OR "DSP Engineer" OR "Audio Engine" at Ableton
- "Director of Engineering" at Bitwig
- "CTO" OR "Head of Technology" at FL Studio / Image-Line
- "Audio DSP" at Native Instruments, iZotope, Waves, FabFilter, Plugin Alliance

### Music Tech & Rights Companies
- Founders/CEOs of companies like: 
  - SoundBetter, Splice, LANDR, Ditto Music, CD Baby, TuneCore, Songtrust, Music Gateway
- "Head of Rights" OR "Technology" OR "IP" at music libraries (e.g., Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed, AudioJungle)

### Creator Economy & Investor Targets
- Investors who have invested in music tech or creator tools (search "music tech" + "investor" or specific funds)
- Founders who previously worked at major audio companies and are now building new tools
