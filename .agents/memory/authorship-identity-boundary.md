---
name: Authorship identity boundary
description: The lyric certificate distinguishes tamper-evident editing evidence from proof of who physically typed the words.
---

The current lyric flow can preserve the AI baseline, record edit snapshots, hash imported text, and score final-vs-draft differences. Those records do not prove that the named artist personally typed every change, and a final-text score alone must not be described as defeating paste, reorder, or delete-and-retype gaming.

**Why:** Browser software can attest to account activity and server-observed content, but cannot establish the human identity behind every keystroke without an additional identity or supervised-authorship mechanism.

**How to apply:** Product and partner copy must say “tamper-evident process evidence” or “human creative contribution evidence,” not “proof the artist wrote every character.” Any stricter guarantee needs a separate approved scope.
