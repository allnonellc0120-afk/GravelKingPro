name: Mobile promo reframe
description: Portrait promo exports use a full-view app shell so stage, controls, singers, and captions remain complete.
---

Use a full-view portrait composition based on the responsive GravelKing Pro screens. Preserve the stage card's aspect ratio inside the phone/iPad canvas instead of stretching or center-cropping it. Both singer panels, the teleprompter, stage floor, and controls must remain visible. Rebuild wide landscape captions below the card at portrait-safe sizes. Keep the mastering card separate from the stage composition.

**Why:** Narrow or stretched source crops removed parts of the stage and clipped the original wide captions. The approved reference is the complete responsive app view, including header, content, controls, and navigation context.

**How to apply:** For future phone/iPad promo edits, use the supplied whole-screen app views as the visual shell. Verify early/mid/late stage frames, an active mastering frame, and the end card at native output dimensions before presenting.
