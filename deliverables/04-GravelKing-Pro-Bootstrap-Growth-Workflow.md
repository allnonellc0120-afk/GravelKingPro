# GravelKing Pro — Bootstrapped Customer Acquisition Workflow

*The operating plan for getting paying customers with near-zero ad spend. Owner: founder + Nina. Loop length: 14 days, repeating.*

---

## 0. The One-Sentence Strategy

**Show the write → record → master → certify flow on video, every day, where songwriters already gather — and let the certificate be the reason they pay.**

## 1. Assets We Already Have

- 59-second vertical promo video (JAX → Vocal Booth → Foundry → certificate) — TikTok/Reels/Shorts ready.
- Live product at gravelkingpro.com with free tier (JAX writing + Vocal Booth recording).
- $1.99 certificate impulse unlock — the lowest-friction paid moment in music software.
- Referral program: commission paid only on verified paid invoices.

## 2. The Funnel

```
Short video / post  →  Free JAX session (no card)  →  Artist writes lyrics
      →  "Compose" + "Master" are Pro moments  →  Song finished (emotional peak)
      →  Certificate offered ($1.99 or King)  →  Paid conversion
      →  Artist shares certificate  →  k-factor loop
```

Every stage has exactly one job. Never ask for money before the artist has lyrics they love.

## 3. Weekly Operating Rhythm (repeatable, ~1 hr/day)

| Day | Action | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Post TikTok + Reel + Short (demo clip or screen capture). Reply to every comment. | 45 min |
| Tue | Reddit: one genuine value post or detailed answer in r/Songwriting, r/WeAreTheMusicMakers, r/indieheads (no link-dropping; profile has the link). | 30 min |
| Wed | Facebook groups (independent artists, home recording, songwriting): share a finished-song certificate image + story. | 30 min |
| Thu | TikTok #2: duet/stitch a songwriting-struggle video, show the fix in GravelKing. | 45 min |
| Fri | Post a "certificate reveal" — the most shareable asset we own. | 20 min |
| Sat | Review analytics (which post drove signups?), double down on the winner's format. | 30 min |
| Sun | Rest / batch-record next week's clips. | — |

## 4. Content Rules (what actually converts)

1. **Show, never tell.** Screen recording of a real song going from prompt to certificate. Captions on, hook in first 2 seconds ("This songwriter can prove every word is his.").
2. **Lead with the wound.** "POV: someone claims your song" beats "check out our features."
3. **One CTA only:** "Write your first verse free — link in bio."
4. **Reply to every comment for the first 90 days.** The comment section is the sales floor.
5. **Certificate reveals are the hero format.** A court-ready PDF for a bedroom song is inherently surprising — it is the ad.

## 5. Platform Notes

- **TikTok/Reels/Shorts:** volume game — 4–5 posts/week, vertical, native captions, trending audio only when it fits.
- **Reddit:** credibility game — never lead with the link; answer songwriting questions thoroughly, mention the tool only when it's genuinely the answer. One spammy post can burn the account.
- **Facebook groups:** community game — join 10 indie-artist groups, be helpful for a week before the first product mention.

## 6. Referral Loop (month 2+)

- Every certificate PDF carries "Certified on GravelKing Pro" — the document is the referral.
- Creators with audiences get the referral link; commission paid only on verified paid invoices (anti-fraud already built).
- Target: 5 micro-influencers (1K–50K followers in songwriting niches) by day 60.

## 7. Measurement (weekly, 15 minutes)

| Metric | Source | Target by day 90 |
|---|---|---|
| Signups/week | app analytics | 150 |
| Free→paid conversion | Stripe/Play | 4% |
| Certificate attach rate | certs issued / songs finished | 30% |
| Cost per paying customer | spend / new subs | < $15 |
| Payback period | CAC / contribution margin | < 2 months |

## 8. When to Spend Money

Only boost a post that has **already proven itself organically** (top 10% by watch-through). $5/day for 3 days against lookalike songwriter interests. Kill anything that doesn't produce signups under $15 each. Never advertise a feature — advertise the finished song and its certificate.
