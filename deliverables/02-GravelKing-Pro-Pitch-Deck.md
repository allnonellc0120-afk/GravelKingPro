## Slide 2 — The Problem
- Independent artists can't prove they wrote their own songs.
- Mastering costs $50–$150 a track; DAWs take months to learn.
- Writing, recording, mastering, and proof live in four different tools.
- AI-music disputes are exploding — and artists have no receipt.

## Slide 3 — The Insight
The moment an artist finishes a song is the most emotional moment in music.
That's when they need two things: **it has to sound like a record, and they have to be able to prove it's theirs.** Nobody delivers both in one flow.

## Slide 4 — The Product (4 tools, one flow)
1. **JAX** — AI songwriting companion with keystroke-level authorship capture.
2. **Vocal Booth** — record, clip, splice, layer, pan. No DAW learning curve.
3. **The Foundry** — Morris Law Kernel v3.5 mastering to streaming loudness.
4. **Converter** — every format your release needs.
Then: **IP Provenance Certificate** — court-ready PDF, HMAC-signed, watermarked into the audio itself.

## Slide 5 — The Demo (30 seconds)
Prompt JAX → refine lyrics with authorship scoring → compose the track → cut vocals in Vocal Booth → master in the Foundry → download the certificate.
*(This is exactly the flow in our 59-second promo video.)*

## Slide 6 — Why the Certificate Wins
- Split-key HMAC-SHA256: half the signature is watermarked into the track, half never leaves our server. Unforgeable client-side.
- Authorship score 0–100 from a keystroke ledger — delete-and-retype scores zero.
- AI model ID + copyright-clearance result printed on the document.
- This is the receipt the industry is about to start demanding.
