Event coverage warning

Traffic interpretation

Pricing reached 27 visitors, but only 3 plan-selection events and 2 checkout starts were recorded. That points to a pricing-page decision or instrumentation gap before checkout.

Privacy and data-deletion account for 118 pageviews combined, which is unusually high relative to pricing. Some of that may be automated or compliance/reviewer traffic rather than buying intent.

Mastering and songwriting are the strongest product pages; they are the best places to connect explicit paywall and CTA tracking.
